About Gudkov Media

Gudkov Media is an independent media and consulting business based in Buenos Aires, Argentina, operated by Maksim Gudkov, an independent consultant registered in Argentina.

We create educational content about relationships and help adults build calmer and more respectful relationships through individual coaching consultations and online programs. Our audience is Russian-speaking and lives in many different countries and time zones.

How we communicate with our audience

Most people discover us through short videos on social media. When someone is interested, they write to us first — usually a keyword such as a request for a free guide or for information about a consultation. To answer quickly in different time zones, we use automated messaging assistance that sends informational replies to people who contacted us first. We never send unsolicited messages.

Our principles

  • Respect and privacy for every person who writes to us.
  • Honest information about our services and prices.
  • Educational coaching only — no medical or clinical claims.
  • Personal data is used only to answer the person's own request.
