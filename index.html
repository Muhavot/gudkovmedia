Coaching and educational content on building harmonious relationships

Gudkov Media is an independent media and consulting business from Buenos Aires, Argentina. We create educational videos and guides and provide individual online coaching consultations for adults who want calmer, warmer and more respectful relationships.

Our servicesContact us

What we offer

All services are delivered online, in Russian, to adult clients worldwide.

Individual consultations

One-to-one online coaching sessions on communication, trust and everyday relationship challenges.

Online programs

Structured multi-day programs with daily tasks and personal support, focused on practical habits in relationships.

Free guides

Short practical guides with exercises that readers can use on their own.

Educational videos

Short videos on our social media channels with simple, practical ideas about communication and relationships.

How it works

Most people find us through our short educational videos on social media.

Watch and learn

You watch our free videos and read our guides.

Send us a message

If you want more, you write to us — for example, a keyword under a video or in direct messages.

Get information

We reply with information about the guide, program or consultation you asked for.

Start working together

If you decide to book, we agree on a convenient time and hold the session online.

Our approach

We focus on practical, respectful and non-judgmental support. Our coaching is educational: it helps clients reflect, communicate better and make their own decisions. It is not medical or psychiatric treatment and is not a substitute for emergency help.
