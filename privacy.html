Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gudkov Media (“we”, “us”), operated by Maksim Gudkov in Buenos Aires, Argentina, handles personal information when you visit this website, contact us, or interact with our business accounts on social media platforms, including TikTok.

1. Information we receive

  • When you message us or comment on our content: your platform user ID, username, and the text of your message or comment.
  • When you email us: your email address and the content of your email.
  • When you book a service: the details you choose to share with us to arrange the session.

We do not collect passwords or payment card details through social media messages.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to reply to your request, provide the materials or services you asked for, arrange consultations, and maintain and improve our service. Automated replies may be prepared with the help of an AI language model; message text may be processed by our AI service provider solely for this purpose.

3. Legal basis

We process your information because you contacted us and asked for information or a service, and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate (hosting, messaging and AI processing providers), and when required by law.

5. Retention

We keep conversation data only as long as needed to answer you and provide our services, and then delete or anonymize it.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing developers@gudkovmedia.com. You may also stop the conversation at any time.

7. Children

Our services are intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will publish the new version on this page.
