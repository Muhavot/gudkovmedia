Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gudkov Media ("we", "us") handles information when you interact with our business accounts and applications on social media platforms, including TikTok.

1. Information we receive

When you send us a message or comment on our business account, we receive your platform user identifier, username, and the text of your message or comment. We do not receive your password or payment card details.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to respond to your messages, provide the information or materials you requested, and operate and improve our service. Replies may be generated with the help of an AI language model; message text may be processed by our AI service provider solely for this purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate the service (hosting and AI processing), and when required by law.

4. Retention

We keep conversation data only as long as needed to provide the service and then delete or anonymize it.

5. Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time by emailing developers@gudkovmedia.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page.
