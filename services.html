Services

All services are provided online, in Russian, to adults (18+).

Individual coaching consultation

A one-to-one online session (video call) where we look at the client's situation in relationships, clarify goals and choose practical next steps. Typical topics: communication with a partner, conflicts and tension at home, rebuilding trust, personal boundaries, confidence in relationships.

  • Format: online video call
  • Duration: about 60 minutes
  • Booking: by message, after a short introduction

Individual online program

A structured program over several days with daily tasks, short exercises and personal feedback in a messenger. Suitable for clients who prefer to work step by step at their own pace.

Free guides

Short practical guides with exercises about communication and relationships. A user can request a guide by sending us a keyword in messages.

Educational video content

We regularly publish short educational videos on our social media accounts, including TikTok (@bron.xsoul0).

Prices and payment

Current prices are sent on request before booking. Payment is made only after the client confirms the booking.

Important

Our services are educational coaching. They are not medical, psychiatric or psychological treatment. If you are in crisis or in danger, please contact your local emergency services.
